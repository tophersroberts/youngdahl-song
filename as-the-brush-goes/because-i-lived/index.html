As The Brush Goes · Reading 23

Because I lived

I worked hard and prepared
as if I would live for a thousand years, and
before I knew it, I turned 30\.

Thinking I would live for a
hundred years, I struggled so hard that I couldn’t
even provide for my children, and
before I knew it, I was over 40\.

Thinking that I should
do something before turning 60,
even if it wasn’t a big deal, I
spent day and night thinking about this and that, and before
I knew it, I had well passed 50\.

I treated the rice cakes for the 60th birthday celebration as if they were someone else’s business, but before they could even all go down my throat

People were whispering because I’m seventy.
Now there is no problem
Are you in your 80s?

I really have to live for a hundred years now.
Not like cancer.
That way, you can try things you haven’t been able to.

I’ll go and see things I haven’t seen, and learn what I don’t know.
Don’t you think so?

Oh my god,
A hundred years is too short.
I wish it were a thousand years.

At the Florida golf course where all the snowbirds that came down from the north have left.
The green mountains without butterflies are desolate and empty; as fine rain falls in late autumn, even the golf course is like an empty mountain.

If there are no people and no feelings, it is Cheongsando Mountain.
