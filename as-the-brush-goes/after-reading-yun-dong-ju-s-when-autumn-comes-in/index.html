As The Brush Goes · Reading 29

After reading Yun Dong-ju’s *When Autumn Comes in…

If winter
approaches my life one day when
heavy snow covers the sky in
gray, I must first feel nothing but gratitude.

One warm late spring day, watching
the cherry blossom petals fluttering
and falling like snow outside the classroom window of Seoul
Middle School, I fell in love with a maiden whose
cheeks were pink.

On that summer day, when cicadas—having dug
up from the ground after seventeen years and had to find love before two weeks had
passed—were crying out at the top of their lungs, I
was on the hill behind Yonsei University

I also agonized alone, reciting, "May I look up
at the sky without a single trace of shame..."

Under the statue of Benjamin Franklin
Rolling around haphazardly
Stepping on fallen leaves
The absolute correlation with GPA, scholarships, degrees, etc.
Without even thinking to ask back
I wandered around the Penn campus with my tired body.

Today, as I gaze at the sky where stars are brushed by
the wind, I become lost in thought,
reflecting on my sparse gray hair,
the deepening wrinkles under my eyes, and
the bathroom I have to visit often.

My autumn fruit is truly not a substantial fruit that can
command a high price.
Perhaps a roadside seed that no one looks
back upon, large but fleshless and unsightly

I am nothing more than a small persimmon.

but,
but,
I am grateful.

Because I lived my life working hard through the four seasons of the year.
Sowing small seeds, watering them, and some
Even though there were days with thunder and lightning
Watching various flowers bloom in all kinds of colors
spring summer and fall
What heart-pounding days those were.

Sure,
In winter, the whole world will be covered in snow, making it cold
deep into the ground, but
When winter comes to my life,
I would feel nothing but gratitude.

Holding the hands of growing young grandchildren
Watching the brilliant spring approach for those children
What more could you ask for?

Spring, summer, autumn, and winter undoubtedly come and go
in turn; flowers of various colors with different fragrances bloom in every season,
and finally, large and small fruits are buried deep in the ground;
this is also the beginning of a glorious spring, an iron rule, and a promise of nature.

Whether the fruit is big or small,
Whether others see it or not,
whether the fragrance is deep or not.
