As The Brush Goes · Reading 37

Pulling out the net in the morning

We pull in the nets
before the early morning sea fog has even
lifted. One, two, three, and then four halibut with
bellies as white as paper.

Yesterday and
today, Noh Heon’s (North Carolina Heon)
song is enough as a feast of
nature sung in the sea.

I brought my ginseng wine to
slowly raise a glass of wine this late evening,
without rushing. I already miss the many
friends living in the big city who left just yesterday.

If time takes care of us
In mid-October, will a friend come
of his own accord next year to this seaside house where there is nothing but sky,
wind, and clouds?

Hey kid
Just release a couple of halibut into deep water.
Time flies so fast; I
will see it again next year.
