As The Brush Goes · Reading 18

Mountains and sea

Mountains have existed since ancient times.
It is the heart of the sea because it
rose up from the sea.

The sea has been since the beginning
mother of
water
It is the heart of water.

The mountains and the sea
creating, feeding, caring for,
and embracing all kinds of life,
Finally, it puts you to sleep.

The mountains and the sea
I don’t reject everything

Accept it silently
asking for nothing
I give it back without a word.

The mountains
and the sea do not
rest day or night,
yet they do not
move a single step.

Because the
mountains and the sea are of their
own accord, they stand there
watching over us for ten million years,
unchanged and just as they are.

They tell us to live like that.
They say to live like a
mountain, like the sea.
